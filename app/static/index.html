    What to look for

    • However many buyers you send, the stock never goes below 0, and the unit chips always add up to the total.
    • Retry same request returns the same order. No second unit is taken.
    • Send same callback again is ignored as a duplicate.
    • Orders of several units are all-or-nothing. When one warehouse is short, the order is split across warehouses.
    • Orders nobody pays for expire and the units go back on sale.
    • Cancel & refund on a paid order puts the units back on sale at once, then the refund finishes a moment later.
    • After orders expire, Send late payments sends the money to the refund list instead of selling stock that is gone.
